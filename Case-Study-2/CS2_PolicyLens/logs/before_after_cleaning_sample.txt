Section 3.2: Product-Wise Cash Disbursement Ceilings

3.1 General Principle of Digital Disbursement
All Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) shall disburse approved loan proceeds directly into the verified
bank account of the borrower via NEFT, RTGS, or IMPS. Physical currency disburse-
ment is strictly restricted to the statutory thresholds specified in Table 3.1 below.

3.2 Statutory Exceptions and Cash Caps
In accordance with Section 269SS of the Income Tax Act and RBI Fair Practices Code, cash payouts across the
counter are permitted only up to Rs. 19,999 for Gold Loans and Rs. 25,000 for rural micro-
finance emergency loans. Any disbursement exceeding these limits must be executed exclusively through
electronic clearing mandates.
